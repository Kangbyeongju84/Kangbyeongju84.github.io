ShhScan Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · App: ShhScan (com.kang.shhscan)

1. Information we collect

ShhScan has no sign-up or login, and never sends your photos, videos or edits to any server.

Ads: The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads. For advertising, Google may collect and process your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (based on IP address) and ad interaction data. See Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. If you buy "Remove ads" in the app's Settings, no ads are shown.

Payments: The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. ShhScan never receives your card or payment details.

2. Where your photos are stored

Photos and edit information are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot access this storage, and it is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Files are saved to your device's Download or gallery folders only when you choose "PDF" or "Save to gallery"; these files are visible to other apps. Videos recorded in Video mode are saved without sound (the microphone is never used) directly to your gallery folder (Pictures/ShhScan), where other apps can see them. Sharing to other apps happens only when you choose to share.

3. Permissions

Camera: Used only to photograph documents and books. The camera runs only while the app's camera screen is open.

Internet and advertising ID: Used only to show ads and verify purchases. Never used to send your photos.

Vibration: Used for a short vibration when a photo is taken (can be turned off in Settings).

4. PIN lock

If you use the PIN lock, the PIN itself is never stored; only an irreversible form (hash) is kept on your device.

5. Deleting your data

Deleting pages in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes all photos and settings in the app's private storage. Files you exported to Download or the gallery must be deleted by you.

6. Permitted use

ShhScan is intended only for photographing documents and study materials. It must not be used to photograph people without their consent; the person taking the photo is legally responsible for such use.
