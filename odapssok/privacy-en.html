Redo Note Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026 · App: Redo Note (com.kang.odapssok) · Operator: Kang Byeongju (KBJ Studio), individual developer

1. Overview

Redo Note is a study app for photographing problems you got wrong, organizing them, and turning them into printable worksheet PDFs. This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Personal information the app collects

None. There is no sign-up or login, the developer runs no server, and your photos and notes are never sent to the developer.

3. Information stored only on your device

Problem and solution photos, subjects, units, mistake reasons, answers, notes, review history and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot access it, and it is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Photos picked from your gallery are re-saved as a new cropped image, so extra photo data such as location (EXIF) is not kept.

4. Permissions

Camera: used only to photograph problems and solutions while the camera screen is open. If you deny it, you can still pick photos from your gallery.

Photo picker: the app uses the Android photo picker, so it can access only the photos you choose. No storage permission is requested.

Internet and advertising ID: used only to show ads and verify purchases.

Vibration: used only for a short vibration when a photo is taken.

5. Files you export yourself

Worksheet PDFs are saved to the Download/OdapSsok folder on your device and are visible to other apps. "Share" and "Make a backup file (zip)" send data only to the app or location you choose. Backup files contain your photos and notes, so keep them safe. Worksheets are for personal study only.

6. Ads (Google AdMob) and consent

The free version shows Google AdMob ads (a banner at the bottom of some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after making a worksheet, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch). For ads, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set IDServing ads, measuring ads, fraud prevention
Approximate location estimated from IP addressServing ads, fraud prevention
App interactions such as ad views and taps, diagnosticsServing and analyzing ads, quality

The app limits ad content to the PG rating. See Google's advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy. Where consent is required (e.g. the EEA), a Google consent form is shown, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings > "Ad privacy settings".

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device settings. Buying "Remove ads + Premium" in the app turns ads off.

7. Purchases and ratings

"Remove ads + Premium" is processed by Google Play Billing; the developer never receives your card or payment details. In-app rating prompts are handled by Google Play.

8. Sharing and international transfer

The developer does not share your personal information with third parties. The Google AdMob and Google Play services included in the app send the data in sections 6 and 7 to Google LLC (USA) for processing while you use the app online.

9. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you delete mistakes in the app or uninstall the app. PDFs in your Download folder and backup files you made must be deleted by you.

10. Children

Redo Note is intended for middle and high school students and exam takers aged 13 and over, and it does not collect personal information directly. Parents or guardians can reduce ad-related processing by purchasing ad removal or turning off personalized ads on the device.

11. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage. Only worksheet PDFs you create are shared with other apps, and only temporarily through a FileProvider.
